ATOMIC BRAND IDENTITY
Recorded 3 November 2026

APPROVED LOGO
Primary wordmark: lowercase atomic.
Typeface: Inter.
Style and weight: SemiBold, 600.
Verified matching release: Inter 3.19, font version 3.019.
Symbol: the supplied overlapping purple/coral gradient symbol.
Decision: retain the current supplied logo. The lighter wordmark study was
reviewed and rejected on 3 November 2026; it is not an approved brand variant.

SOURCE OF TRUTH
Use the supplied outlined SVG artwork, rather than retyping the name.
Original masters live in dist/assets/brand/originals/:

- atomic-logo-malo-dark.svg: lowercase wordmark with light lettering.
- atomic-znak.svg: standalone gradient symbol.

The outlined SVGs render without an installed font. Preserve the original
letter shapes, spacing, proportions and symbol when exporting other formats.
The launch-film closing lockup uses the monochrome white symbol and wordmark
on purple. Use the four separated shapes and transparent gaps specified below.

TYPEFACE VERIFICATION
All glyph segments and control points in both supplied wordmarks were
compared against the official Inter SemiBold font from Inter 3.19. They match
after scale and translation within the SVG's rounded coordinate precision
(maximum discrepancy below 0.061 SVG units).
Official font source: https://github.com/rsms/inter/releases/tag/v3.19
The detailed measurements and source-file hashes are retained alongside this
file in logo-font-verification.json.

PREVIOUS IDENTITY AND WEBSITE TYPOGRAPHY
The previous website logo paired the purple orbit symbol with lowercase
atomic in Manrope 600, using letter spacing -0.075em. It was a different
font family despite sharing the 600 weight label. The old launch film used
a separate Manrope 750 text treatment; do not use that as the old-logo reference.
Manrope remains website/campaign text typography and must not be mistaken
for the current logo's Inter SemiBold wordmark.

MONOCHROME LOGO — APPROVED REFERENCE, 3 NOVEMBER 2026
Black and white monochrome marks must preserve four distinct shapes with
transparent gaps at the overlaps, as shown in the supplied black reference.
The gap reveals the background; it is not a white stroke painted into the logo.
Never use a merged solid silhouette, brightness/invert recolouring of the
gradient mark, or a monochrome conversion that removes the overlap gaps.
Masters: dist/assets/brand/monochrome/atomic-symbol-black.svg and -white.svg;
complete lowercase lockups: atomic-lowercase-black.svg and -white.svg.
Both colours use identical geometry. The Inter SemiBold 600 wordmark stays
unchanged. Colour and dark-background colour variants remain separate assets.
