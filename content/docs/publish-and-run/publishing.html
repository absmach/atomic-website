Try the complete workflow

Start with a new user and follow the main task to its conclusion. Check confirmation messages, error recovery, and what an administrator sees afterward. Remove sample records and clearly identify any functionality that is still a demonstration.

Check identity and permissions

Test with separate accounts for customers and administrators. Verify that users can access only the records and actions intended for them. Hiding a button is not a substitute for enforcing permissions on the backend.

Review content and accessibility

Replace placeholder text, images, prices, and contact details. Test keyboard navigation, form labels, readable contrast, and small screens. Ensure the purpose of each button and the result of each action are clear.

Prepare for real data

Decide what data you actually need, who can access it, and how long you will keep it. Provide the notices and terms appropriate to your app. Atomic’s policies cover Atomic’s service; they do not automatically cover the relationship between your business and its customers.

Confirm the supported backup, deletion, and export processes before collecting important records. Avoid sensitive or regulated data unless your agreement and deployment explicitly support it.

Know how you will operate it

Confirm hosting limits, any external service charges, your support contact, and the process for responding to problems. Review the published release after deployment. Frontend rollback changes the release served to visitors; it does not rewind business data.
