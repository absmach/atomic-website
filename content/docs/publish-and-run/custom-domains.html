Current availability

Custom domains are planned for paid subscriptions. The current early-access interface does not provide a custom-domain connection workflow. Publish to the Atomic address shown for your app.

Prepare for a custom domain

You may register a domain with a registrar of your choice. Registration is separate from Atomic. Wait for supported connection instructions before changing DNS records; no CNAME or TXT value is supplied by this guide.

What will be documented

Supported domain types, ownership verification, DNS records, certificate management and plan limits will be documented when the feature is available. Do not rely on automatic connection or renewal before that release.
