Choose one useful job

Start with a task you can describe in a sentence. A booking request, a small product catalog, or a client project view gives you a focused first version. Save the larger wish list for later.

For example: “Help my clients request a design consultation without a long email exchange.” That is specific enough to test with someone who might use it.

Write a practical brief

Include the people using the app, the information they provide, and what each person should be allowed to see or change.

Build a booking app for my studio. Clients choose a service and request an available time. I review requests and confirm them. A client should only see their own booking. Start with sample services and sample clients.

Add your preferred tone and appearance after the workflow is clear. Say whether the app should feel calm, playful, minimal, or editorial, and describe any existing brand assets you want to use.

Define a test before you build

  • Can a client understand the available services?
  • Does submitting a request produce a clear confirmation?
  • Can the owner review and change a request?
  • Can one client access another client’s information?
  • What happens when a time is unavailable or a field is empty?

A useful brief includes the difficult cases as well as the happy path.
