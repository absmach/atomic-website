Data

  • Restoring a version or rolling back a deployment changes code, never data. A published change to how data is stored is not undone by either.
  • A source download is not a complete export: it leaves out users, records, uploaded files and hosting.
  • Apps do not share a database, and outside tools cannot connect to an app’s data directly.
  • Anything you create in the preview is discarded; publishing does not copy it.
  • In a public app with no login, any visitor could read or change any record.

People and access

  • A published app starts with no users. Sample accounts are never published.
  • App users can be listed and invited only after the app is published.
  • Your email address cannot be changed from Settings yet.
  • Workspace roles cannot be changed, and ownership cannot be transferred, from the workspace page yet.

Building and the canvas

  • Apps started from a template, or shaped on the canvas, must be applied before they can be published.
  • Figma links are not read; attach a .fig file or SVG frames. Reading .fig files is experimental.
  • Image attachments: up to four PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF images of up to 5 MB each.
  • Text edited in place stays plain text, up to 600 bytes. Elements containing controls or icons are changed through annotations.
  • Saved screens do not replay clicks, form entries or sign-ins.
  • Atomic keeps an app’s newest 20 versions, plus the published one.

Publishing and running

  • Custom domains are subdomains only, on paid plans.
  • Logs cover the preview, only while it is open, and keep each app’s newest 2,000 lines.
  • Connections to external services and Testing appear in app settings as coming later.
