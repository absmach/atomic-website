What credits measure

Building uses AI models, and Atomic counts that work in credits. Builds, Plan answers, design suggestions and dictation all use credits, according to the work they take. Not every request costs the same. Starting an app from a template uses none; applying its screens afterwards runs a build like any other.

Your plan includes credits for each period. Included credits start over each period and do not roll over. Some plans also spread them across the days of the period, so you cannot spend a whole month on its first day. Where credit packs are offered, you can buy more.

Plan names, prices and allowances are not final. See Pricing for the current proposal.

When credits run out

Atomic sets aside what a build may need before it starts. When there are not enough credits left, new builds are refused until your credits reset or you add more. A build already running is not stopped. Your apps, and your published apps, keep working.

See where they went

Usage in the sidebar shows what every app on your account has used over the last 7, 30 or 90 days: how many builds started, and where the credits went, heaviest first. A deleted app keeps what it used. Where billing is enabled, the page also offers credit packs and a link to your payment methods and receipts.

Each app has its own usage page too: open the app’s Dashboard and select See usage for its recent build sessions, with the credits and time each took. The Builds page shows what each individual build used.

Your plan

Settings shows your plan, the credits left this period and when they reset, with a way to upgrade where it is offered. Plans can also limit things other than credits, such as how many apps you have, how many are published at once, collaborators per app, and custom domains.

Work on an app is counted to the app’s owner, so building in someone else’s app uses their credits, not yours.
