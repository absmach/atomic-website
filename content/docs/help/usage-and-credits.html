Usage and proposed credits

Generation uses AI model resources. The current early-access usage view reports build activity and model tokens. A token count is not a fixed number of messages, a successful app, or a currency amount.

The proposed launch plans use a separate credit offer: 250 credits once on Free, 900 monthly on Builder and 3,900 monthly on Pro, with 100 credits representing $1 of Atomic usage. Unused monthly credits would carry over for one month; purchased top-ups would last 12 months and be consumed after monthly credits. These are proposed commercial rules, not a conversion applied to every early-access installation. Subscriptions are not open on this website.

Metered hosting and storage are also part of the proposal. Their rates and limits must be included in the final offer before billing starts. Starting from a template without generation does not itself call an AI model; later generation requests consume resources.

When an allowance runs out

When the current allowance is insufficient, Atomic can refuse new generation requests. Account settings show the applicable limits and renewal information where billing is enabled. Funding and retention of running apps follow the applicable offer; unused generation allowance is not a promise of unlimited hosting.

The final offer will describe hosting suspension, restart, retention and warning arrangements. Keep separate copies of important data; source downloads do not include business records.

Review usage

Usage in the sidebar shows activity over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, including builds, model tokens and tokens read from cache. Missing usage reports are identified separately. The current view does not establish a monetary hosting charge. Billing-enabled installations may show additional purchase and billing controls.

Open an app’s Dashboard and select See usage for its recorded generation activity. The Builds page provides details of individual build sessions. Usage history may retain activity attributed to a deleted app.

Your plan

Settings shows generation usage and any allowance controls supplied by the installation’s billing configuration. The planned Free, Builder and Pro offers on the website are not yet the rules of every early-access account.

In that proposal, Free includes solo building and a backend with database and authentication for every app. Builder and Pro add collaboration, source downloads and searchable logs; Pro adds the choice of a stronger model. Custom domains are planned. Final resource and billing limits will be supplied before a subscription is accepted.

Generation on a shared app is attributed to the app owner’s account. See the applicable offer before using shared resources.
