Building

What you seeWhat to do
A build stays queuedIt is waiting for capacity and will start on its own. Check its state on the Builds page.
Not verifiedThe build failed a check. Read the conversation for the reason and ask for a fix. The previous verified version is still there.
Atomic offers “Change the backend”Your request changed nothing on the screens. Accept if it was about how the app stores or handles data.
New builds are refusedYou may be out of credits for now. See when credits run out.
The preview is blank or staleUse the reload icon in the top bar. Check logs for errors from the app.

Publishing

What you seeWhat to do
Publish is greyed outThe latest version must be verified, no build may be running, and you must be the owner or an admin.
“Apply the screens first”The app still runs on sample data. Select Apply to connect its backend, wait for Verified, then publish.
The published app differs from the previewLater changes stay in the preview until you publish again. Check the live deployment in the app’s settings.
Nobody can sign in to the published appSample accounts are not published. Invite your first admin and your app users.
A custom domain will not connectCheck both DNS records, keep the TXT record, and turn off any DNS proxy until it connects. See custom domains.

Accounts and invitations

What you seeWhat to do
An invitation cannot be acceptedSign in with the invited email address and make sure it is verified. Invitations to apps last seven days and work once; ask for a new one if it has expired.
The verification email did not arriveUse Verify your email or request a new link on the log-in page.
“Invite user” is not availableApp users are kept by the published app. Publish first, and choose open sign-up or invite-only access.
A reconnecting banner keeps appearingRefresh the page once. If it continues, contact the team with the page address and the time.

Reporting a problem

Include the app’s ID (shown in the app’s Dashboard) or the workspace ID, the time, what you did and the exact message you saw. Logs downloaded from the app help too. Never send passwords, invitation links or other secrets.

Contact the Atomic team at info@absmach.eu.
