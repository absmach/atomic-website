Brand guide

Present Atomic clearly.

Use the supplied gradient symbol and outlined wordmark. The primary lockup in this kit is lowercase “atomic”; the uppercase alternative is included.

Colour and artwork

The symbol blends indigo #4338CA, violet #7C72FF, coral #FF8A6B and magenta #C95BB4. Light-background lettering is #16161D. Preserve the supplied gradients, overlap and shading.

Choose a background

Use the colour versions on light or dark backgrounds. For one-colour use, choose the supplied black or white monochrome SVG. Its four separate shapes and transparent gaps preserve the overlap; never flatten the symbol into a silhouette.

Space and legibility

Leave clear space of at least half the symbol’s height around the logo. As a starting point, use the lockup at 120 pixels wide or larger and the symbol at 24 pixels or larger; check legibility at final size.

Keep the identity intact

The lowercase wordmark is Inter SemiBold (600), verified against Inter 3.19. Keep its supplied weight, letter shapes and spacing. Manrope is the document and campaign typeface. Write “Atomic” in editorial text.
