Drasko Draskovic

Founder & CEO · Paris, France

Drasko Draskovic is the founder of Abstract Machines and the founder and CEO behind Atomic. A deep-tech entrepreneur and systems engineer, he brings more than 20 years of experience in IoT and cloud-computing systems. His background includes work at Texas Instruments, Alcatel-Lucent, Nokia, Sequans and Devialet. He co-founded Datapace as an internal venture at Nokia, where he served as CTO. His work spans open-source infrastructure and the identity, data and execution systems that support applications. LinkedIn · X
