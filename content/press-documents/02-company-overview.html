Company overview

The team behind Atomic.

Abstract Machines is a Paris-based applied AI company building intelligent software and the infrastructure behind it. Its work combines application development, open-source engineering and research across connected systems, identity and confidential computing. Atomic is its AI app builder; Atom is the open-source backend providing identity, permissions and application data.

Engineering behind the product

The team’s background spans connected systems, identity, cloud and edge software. Its open-source work includes Magistrala, an IoT platform, alongside European research in orchestration, confidential computing and security.

Atomic, Atom and Abstract Machines

Atomic is the AI app builder. Atom is the open-source backend for identities, permissions and data. Abstract Machines is the company developing both, alongside its wider engineering and research work.
