Product announcement

Turn ideas into applications.

Abstract Machines announces an AI app builder for founders, freelancers and small businesses.

Abstract Machines introduces Atomic, an AI app builder for people who know what they want to create. Atomic helps turn plain-language ideas into web applications, refine them through conversation and visual editing, and publish a version they have reviewed.

Users can begin with a prompt or a template. The Canvas brings screens and styling controls together, while contextual comments keep feedback attached to the part of an app being discussed.

Product announcement

From an idea to a reviewed release

Atomic combines a working preview, a visual Canvas, source access and version history. Users can adjust whole-app styling, refine individual elements and work with teammates. Generated applications use Atom, the open-source backend from the same team, for identities, permissions and business data.

“We built Atomic for people who know what their business needs but don’t want to start with a blank code editor. Describe the app, shape it with AI, and make it something your customers can use.”Drasko Draskovic, Founder & CEO

Product information

Proposed plans: Free ($0), Builder $25/month, Pro $50/month. Subscriptions are not open; final consumer totals include applicable taxes. Visit atomicapp.dev for product information and access details.

About Abstract Machines

Abstract Machines is a Paris-based applied AI company building intelligent software and the infrastructure behind it. Its work combines application development, open-source engineering and research across connected systems, identity and confidential computing. Atomic is its AI app builder; Atom is the open-source backend providing identity, permissions and application data.
