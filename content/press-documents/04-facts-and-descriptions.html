Atomic, at a glance.

One sentence

Atomic is an AI app builder from Abstract Machines that turns plain-language ideas into web applications, with visual editing, previews, source access and publishing.

Short description

Atomic helps founders, freelancers and small businesses turn an idea into a web application. Start with a prompt or template, refine the result in conversation and on the Canvas, inspect the source, and publish a reviewed version. Atom, the open-source backend from the same team, provides application identities, permissions and data.

Company boilerplate

Abstract Machines is a Paris-based applied AI company building intelligent software and the infrastructure behind it. Its work combines application development, open-source engineering and research across connected systems, identity and confidential computing. Atomic is its AI app builder; Atom is the open-source backend providing identity, permissions and application data.
