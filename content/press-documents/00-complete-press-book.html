Press FAQ

Questions worth answering.

What is Atomic?

Atomic helps founders, freelancers and small businesses turn an idea into a web application. Start with a prompt or template, refine the result in conversation and on the Canvas, inspect the source, and publish a reviewed version. Atom, the open-source backend from the same team, provides application identities, permissions and data.

Do I need to know how to code?

Start by describing the app you want. Review the preview and refine it through conversation or visual editing. You should still test the workflows before sharing it.

What can I build?

Booking apps, online shops, client portals and internal tools are typical starting points. The commerce starter records order requests; it does not by itself collect payments.

What does the Canvas do?

Navigate screens and states, select elements, tune whole-app styling and leave contextual comments. Builder and Pro support shared workspaces; workspace builders and finished-app users have separate accounts and access flows.

Press FAQ

Questions worth answering.

Can I inspect and download the code?

All plans support source browsing. Source downloads are included in the proposed Builder and Pro plans. An archive contains code, not app users, business data or hosting infrastructure; it is not a complete portable deployment package.

What does “Verified” mean?

It identifies a version that passed the configured automated checks, including build and browser smoke checks. It is not a guarantee that every business workflow is correct or an independent security audit.

Can I restore an earlier version?

Source history and publication history concern code. Restoring source or rolling back a release does not restore business data; publication rollback also depends on compatibility with applied data migrations.

Is Atomic open source?

Atom, the backend for identities, permissions and application data, is open source. This does not mean the Atomic builder itself is open source.

What plans does Atomic offer?

Proposed plans: Free ($0), Builder $25/month, Pro $50/month. Subscriptions are not open; final consumer totals include applicable taxes. Free includes 250 credits once, with no daily limit. Source downloads, searchable preview logs and collaboration are included in the proposed paid plans. Product information: atomicapp.dev.

Brand guide

Present Atomic clearly.

Use the supplied gradient symbol and outlined wordmark. The primary lockup in this kit is lowercase “atomic”; the uppercase alternative is included.

Colour and artwork

The symbol blends indigo #4338CA, violet #7C72FF, coral #FF8A6B and magenta #C95BB4. Light-background lettering is #16161D. Preserve the supplied gradients, overlap and shading.

Choose a background

Use the colour versions on light or dark backgrounds. For one-colour use, choose the supplied black or white monochrome SVG. Its four separate shapes and transparent gaps preserve the overlap; never flatten the symbol into a silhouette.

Space and legibility

Leave clear space of at least half the symbol’s height around the logo. As a starting point, use the lockup at 120 pixels wide or larger and the symbol at 24 pixels or larger; check legibility at final size.

Keep the identity intact

The lowercase wordmark is Inter SemiBold (600), verified against Inter 3.19. Keep its supplied weight, letter shapes and spacing. Manrope is the document and campaign typeface. Write “Atomic” in editorial text.
